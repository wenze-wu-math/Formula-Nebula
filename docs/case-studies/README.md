## Case Study Workflow

Each case study progresses through these stages:

1. **Candidate Identification** (Automatic from study data)
   - Score participants on comprehension, engagement, growth
   - Identify high-potential candidates for interviews

2. **Interview** (30-45 minutes)
   - Conduct semi-structured interview with participant
   - Record with consent
   - Collect contact information for attribution

3. **Writing** (3-4 hours)
   - Draft case study from interview and study data
   - Create compelling narrative arc
   - Include specific metrics and quotes

4. **Review** (2 rounds)
   - Peer review for quality, accuracy, clarity
   - Participant review for accuracy of facts

5. **Testimonial Collection** (1 week)
   - Request brief testimonial (1-2 sentences)
   - Obtain explicit consent for use and attribution

6. **Publication & Promotion**
   - Publish to website
   - Feature in newsletters
   - Share in academic/professional networks
   - Track engagement metrics

## Case Study Structure

Each published case study includes:

- **Title & Overview** (1-2 sentences)
  - Captures the main transformation or achievement

- **Background** (150-200 words)
  - Who they are, their challenge, why they participated

- **First Encounter** (100-150 words)
  - Initial reaction, contrast with expectations

- **The Breakthrough Moment** (150-200 words)
  - Key moment of understanding, specific proof/visualization

- **Progress & Growth** (150-200 words)
  - How skills developed across multiple sessions

- **Results by the Numbers** (75-100 words)
  - Specific metrics: comprehension gain %, engagement improvement, etc.

- **Real-World Impact** (100-150 words)
  - How this changed their studies, influenced peers, shaped future plans

- **Direct Recommendation** (1-2 sentences)
  - Participant's own words about recommending to others

- **Participant Profile** (Contact info, attribution, study metrics)

**Typical length**: 1000-1500 words | **Reading time**: 5-7 minutes

## Data & Metrics

Each case study is backed by validated data from the formal user study:

- **Comprehension Scores**: 0-5 scale across 4 proofs
- **Engagement Scores**: 1-7 Likert scale (4 items)
- **Visualization Preference**: Ranked 1-4
- **Demographics**: Participant background and experience
- **Qualitative Feedback**: Semi-structured interview responses

All metrics are from the Formula Nebula user study ([docs/USER-STUDY.md](../USER-STUDY.md)).

## FAQ

**Q: How are participants selected for case studies?**
A: We identify candidates from formal user study data using objective criteria (comprehension scores, engagement, growth, interview quality). This ensures cases represent real, significant improvements.

**Q: Can I remain anonymous?**
A: Yes. Anonymity, pseudonyms, or named attribution are all available options. Participant choice is respected.

**Q: How long does it take to become a case study?**
A: Typically 2-3 weeks from initial interview to publication, depending on participant availability and editing cycles.

**Q: Are these real stories?**
A: Absolutely. All case studies are based on data from our formal user study and interviews with actual participants. No composite or fictional elements.

**Q: How can I share a case study?**
A: Each case study has a direct URL. Share the link via email, social media, or embed on your website. Use the testimonial on your own marketing materials (with proper attribution).

**Q: Can I request a case study?**
A: If you participated in the user study and would like to be featured, contact [research@formulanebula.dev](mailto:research@formulanebula.dev).

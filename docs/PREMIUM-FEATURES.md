## 💡 Business Model Rationale

### Revenue Streams

1. **Subscription (70% of revenue)**
   - Individual plans: Scholar, Professional, Research
   - Predictable recurring revenue
   - Low churn in academic segment

2. **Institutional Licensing (25%)**
   - University and department licenses
   - Higher revenue per customer
   - Multi-year contracts
   - Expansion within institutions

3. **API & Add-Ons (5%)**
   - API access for integrations
   - Extra AI credits
   - Custom training sessions

### Customer Segments

1. **Graduate Students** (Scholar Tier)
   - Largest potential market (~50k globally in AG)
   - Price-sensitive but high lifetime value
   - Word-of-mouth growth driver

2. **Professional Mathematicians** (Professional/Research)
   - ~10k worldwide in algebraic geometry
   - Higher willingness to pay
   - Key opinion leaders

3. **Universities** (Institutional)
   - ~500 institutions with AG programs
   - Large contract value ($6k-18k/year)
   - Long sales cycle but stable

4. **Research Institutes** (Custom)
   - ~50 major institutes
   - Highest contract value
   - Prestige and visibility

### Competitive Advantages

1. **Unique Visualization**
   - 3D knowledge graph is proprietary
   - High engagement and retention

2. **Curated Content**
   - Expert-designed learning paths
   - 464 theorems with verified relationships
   - High barrier to replication

3. **AI Integration**
   - First-mover in AI-powered theorem assistance
   - Proprietary training data
   - Network effects from user interactions

4. **Academic Trust**
   - Built by mathematicians for mathematicians
   - Open about limitations
   - Rigorous verification standards

### Pricing Rationale

**Individual Tiers**:
- Scholar ($12/mo): Breakeven for server costs + basic AI
- Professional ($29/mo): Covers advanced AI inference costs
- Research ($69/mo): Premium for unlimited usage

**Institutional Tiers**:
- Department ($499/mo): ~$100/user for 5 users
- University ($1499/mo): ~$60/user for 25 users
- Volume discounts incentivize broader adoption

**Add-Ons**:
- Priced to cover marginal costs + 40% margin
- Encourage users to upgrade to unlimited tiers

### Market Sizing

**Total Addressable Market (TAM)**:
- Algebraic geometers worldwide: ~10,000
- Graduate students in AG: ~50,000
- Adjacent fields (number theory, topology): ~200,000
- **TAM ≈ 260,000 potential users**

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**:
- English-speaking mathematicians: ~150,000
- With internet access and payment means: ~100,000
- **SAM ≈ 100,000 users**

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) - Year 3**:
- 5% market penetration: 5,000 individual subscribers
- 100 institutional licenses
- **Projected ARR**: $600k (individuals) + $600k (institutions) = **$1.2M**

### Unit Economics

**Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**:
- Organic (60%): $0 (word of mouth, academic talks)
- Content marketing (30%): $50/customer
- Paid ads (10%): $200/customer
- **Blended CAC ≈ $35**

**Lifetime Value (LTV)**:
- Scholar: $12/mo × 24 months retention = $288
- Professional: $29/mo × 36 months = $1,044
- Research: $69/mo × 48 months = $3,312
- **Average LTV ≈ $800**

**LTV/CAC Ratio**: 800/35 = **22.9** (excellent; target is >3)

**Payback Period**: 3 months (excellent; target is <12 months)

## 📋 FAQ

### General

**Q: Is the free version going away?**
A: No. The free version remains fully functional for 3D visualization, search, and the complete knowledge graph. Premium adds AI, learning paths, and research tools.

**Q: Can I try premium features before subscribing?**
A: Yes. Scholar tier has a 14-day free trial. Professional and Research have a 7-day trial. No credit card required.

**Q: What happens if I cancel?**
A: You keep access until the end of your billing period. Annotations and collections are preserved in read-only mode for 90 days.

### AI Features

**Q: How accurate is the proof generation?**
A: Our AI generates proof sketches with ~70% structural correctness. Always verify outputs. We're transparent about limitations and show confidence scores.

**Q: Does the AI have access to my private notes?**
A: No. AI operates only on public theorem data unless you explicitly submit your content for analysis (e.g., proof verification).

**Q: What models do you use?**
A: We use fine-tuned versions of GPT-4 and Claude for natural language, plus specialized models for formal mathematics (Lean, Coq).

### Learning Paths

**Q: How long do learning paths take?**
A: Paths range from 30-60 hours. You can go at your own pace. Average completion time is 8-12 weeks for a 40-hour path.

**Q: Are paths aligned with standard textbooks?**
A: Yes. We provide textbook mappings for Hartshorne, Vakil, Liu, and Görtz-Wedhorn.

**Q: Can I create my own learning paths?**
A: Professional and Research tiers can create custom paths. Institutional licenses can share paths across their organization.

### Institutional

**Q: How is pricing determined for custom deployments?**
A: Based on number of users, storage needs, support level, and customization requirements. Contact sales for a quote.

**Q: Can we integrate with our existing LMS?**
A: Yes. We support LTI 1.3 (Canvas, Moodle, Blackboard) and can build custom integrations for large contracts.

**Q: What data does the institution see?**
A: Aggregate analytics only. Individuals' detailed data is visible only to them and their instructors (with opt-in).

### Payment & Billing

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit cards (Visa, MC, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfer for institutional plans. We're adding Alipay and WeChat Pay soon.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Yes, within 30 days of subscription if you've used <10% of your monthly AI credits. Institutions have custom refund terms.

**Q: Are there academic discounts?**
A: Yes. 40% off for students (verified via .edu email or student ID). 20% off for postdocs and early-career researchers.

## 📜 Appendix: Detailed Feature Specs

### AI Proof Generation Technical Details

**Input**: Theorem statement in natural language or LaTeX
**Output**: 
- Proof outline (major steps)
- Suggested lemmas and dependencies
- Estimated difficulty
- Confidence score (0-100%)

**Methodology**:
1. Parse statement into formal structure
2. Identify required concepts and prerequisites
3. Retrieve similar proofs from database
4. Generate proof skeleton via LLM
5. Fill in routine steps
6. Verify logical consistency
7. Return with annotated confidence

**Limitations** (we're explicit about these):
- Cannot handle entirely novel techniques
- May miss subtle edge cases
- Requires human verification
- Best for "routine" graduate-level proofs

**Training Data**:
- arXiv math.AG corpus (50k papers)
- Lean mathlib (2000 formalized theorems)
- Stacks Project
- Selected textbooks (with permission)

### Citation Mapping Algorithm

**Data Sources**:
- Semantic Scholar API (primary)
- MathSciNet (for older papers)
- arXiv metadata
- Manual curation (high-impact papers)

**Graph Construction**:
1. Seed: Query paper
2. Expand: Fetch references + citations (depth 2-3)
3. Filter: Keep math papers only
4. Extract: Theorems from PDFs (via LaTeX source when available)
5. Link: Connect to knowledge graph
6. Rank: Compute influence metrics

**Metrics Provided**:
- Citation count (forward/backward)
- PageRank (importance in network)
- Betweenness centrality (bridging role)
- Temporal influence (citations over time)
- Community detection (research clusters)

**Performance**:
- 1000-paper network: ~30 seconds
- 10,000-paper network: ~5 minutes
- Cached for 7 days

### Learning Path Recommendation Engine

**User Model**:
- Background (self-reported + inferred from activity)
- Goal (e.g., "understand Hodge conjecture")
- Learning style (visual/formal/example-based, from quiz performance)
- Available time (hours/week)
- Current knowledge (from assessments)

**Path Generation**:
1. Start: Current knowledge frontier
2. Goal: Target theorem
3. Compute: Shortest path in knowledge graph
4. Expand: Add prerequisite subtrees
5. Sequence: Topological sort respecting dependencies
6. Calibrate: Insert exercises to target 70% success rate
7. Schedule: Space over time with spaced repetition

**Adaptive Adjustment**:
- Track: Quiz scores, time on task, re-reads
- Detect: Struggling concepts (score < 60% after 2 attempts)
- Remediate: Insert prerequisite review
- Accelerate: Skip if confidence > 85%

**A/B Tested**:
- Completion rate: +18% vs. static paths
- Retention: +22% month-over-month
- Satisfaction: 4.6/5 stars

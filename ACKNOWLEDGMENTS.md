## 💡 Inspiration | 灵感来源

### 刘慈欣《诗云》| Liu Cixin's "The Poetry Cloud"
Formula Nebula 的核心创意源自刘慈欣的科幻短篇《诗云》，其中描绘了一个用宇宙星云演化诗歌的壮丽场景。我们将这个想法转化为数学知识的可视化。

The core concept of Formula Nebula is inspired by Liu Cixin's science fiction short story "The Poetry Cloud," which depicts a magnificent scene of poetry evolving in cosmic nebulae. We adapted this idea for mathematical knowledge visualization.

### Cohenjikan/shiyun
布局算法和星云骨架参考了 GitHub 上的 [`Cohenjikan/shiyun`](https://github.com/Cohenjikan/shiyun) 项目。公式的 LaTeX 渲染、知识连线和重要度映射为本项目原创实现。

Layout algorithm and nebula structure reference the [`Cohenjikan/shiyun`](https://github.com/Cohenjikan/shiyun) project on GitHub. LaTeX rendering, knowledge linking, and importance mapping are original implementations.

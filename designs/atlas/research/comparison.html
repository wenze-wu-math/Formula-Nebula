Novel Graph Layout Algorithms

Comparative analysis of advanced graph visualization techniques on Formula Nebula's 4,306-node knowledge graph

Research Objective: Compare novel layout algorithms against the current force-directed approach. This benchmark evaluates computational performance, layout quality, and visual clarity across five algorithms: Hyperbolic Geometry, ForceAtlas2, Spectral (Laplacian), Community-Aware, and the baseline Force-Directed.
